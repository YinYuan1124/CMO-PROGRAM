Briefing

Significant forces are now in position to attack the Chinese homeland. Your objective is to attack several key targets to diminish China’s ability to project power and demonstrate to the Chinese government that the homeland is vulnerable to significant attacks.

ROE

You are to restrict attacks to marked targets and defensive units on the Chinese mainland. A greater air campaign will open up once China's offensive capabilities are reduced.

Weather

Clear

Threat:

The principle offensive threat you face to your forces are the remaining bomber, cruise and ballistic missiles in the Chinese inventory.

The Chinese homeland is the most heavily defended target in history. The Chinese integrated defense system includes modern radar and passive detection systems as well as modern SAM systems. Expect heavy combat air patrols from state of the art Flanker, J-10 and J-20 stealth aircraft as well as significant AEW and EW support. Expect the Navy to screen critical targets with modern destroyers and defend vessels with ASW assets.
