Briefing

Task:

You are to shadow South Vietnamese naval forces. If engaged, you are to defend decisively inflicting greater damage upon the enemy.

Timeline:

You are to move westward immediately.

Threat:

Four Vietnamese combatants known to be operating in the region, that consist of one minesweeper/patrol craft and three frigate/destroyer escort sized patrol combatants. They are:

  • RVNS Ly Thuong Kiet
  • RVNS Nhat Tao
  • RVNS Tran Khanh Du
  • RVNS Tran Binh Trong

They are moving toward your area quickly so command expects offensive action. Their principle advantage beyond greater size is their higher-caliber 76mm and 127mm guns that can target you at a greater range.

Assets:

You have 6 light combatants under your command.

  • 396, 389 (Kronshtadt Patrol Vessels)
  • 271, 274 (T-43 Mine Sweeper Patrol Craft)
  • 281, 282 (Type 037 Hainan Patrol Craft)
