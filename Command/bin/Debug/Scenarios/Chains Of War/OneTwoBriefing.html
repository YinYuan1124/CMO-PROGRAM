Briefing

Task:

Command would like you to get USS Key West and USS North Carolina safely out to sea and into the fight. You must get both subs to the area marked by references points (V1-V4) on your tactical map.

Timeline:

You have 8 hours to accomplish this task.

Threat:

The PLAN knows Apra Harbor is a US submarine base, and will likely try and ambush any submarines exiting port before they disappear into the deeps of the greater Pacific. The best assets to do this are mines and submarines. Multiple Chinese Han or Shang class nuclear attack submarines are the most likely threat given the distances, however Kilo, Song or Yuan diesel attack boats could be involved as well.

Assets:

  • USS Key West
  • USS North Dakota
  • Task Group Baloogan
    • Baloogan ASW Group: 1 Fleet CUSV [ASW Hunter], 2 Fleet CUSV [ASW Killer]
    • Baloogan MW Group: 1 Fleet CUSV [Mine Hunter], 1 Swordfish UUV, 2 Fleet CUSV [Mine Killers]
    • Baloogan Helicopter Mine Hunting Section: 2 MH-60S

Execution:

Task Group Baloogan is a small experimental group currently working on developing tactics to clear the approaches to port of hostile submarines and mines. You are to leverage these assets to get your nuclear boats out to sea.

Key West and North Dakota should proceed carefully behind these assets using traditional sprint and drift tactics to get to the marked area.

Apra Harbor's exit it to the west you should proceed out of port in that direction.
