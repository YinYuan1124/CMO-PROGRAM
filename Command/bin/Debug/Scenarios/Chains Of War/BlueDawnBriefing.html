Briefing

Task:

Your objectives are the destruction of fifteen preplanned targets threatening our island garrisons, followed by ambushing North Korean land and sea assets as they sortie to challenge our forces. Ensure maximum destruction for minimum losses, to achieve the desired punitive effect. Expect pop-up threats from artillery and missiles as they emerge from hidden tunnels in the hills. Airspace likely to be vigorously contested by DPRK Air Forces.

Timeline:

You have 9 hours to accomplish your tasks.

Targets:

Your initial target/aimpoint set includes 15 targets that threaten the Yeonpyong and Baengnyeong Island Garrisons. These include:

  • 130mm artillery batteries and their supporting ammo dumps.
  • KN-01 surface to surface missile batteries and their supporting radars.

They have all been marked as [Target] on your tactical map.

Command expects the DPRK to counter attack as they are able. You are to monitor the North Korean coastline and destroy any emerging threats with a focus on any units able to inflict harm on South Korean forces and civilians.

Threat:

The current DPRK air threat includes mostly obsolete SA-2, SA-3, MiG-21PFM Fishbed, F-6A Farmer, F-5A Fresco and MiG-23ML weapon systems, except around Pyongyang which is defended by more modern MiG-29B Fulcrum fighters and an extremely modern KN-06 SAM system. There are also significant numbers of low-level AAA and man-portable SAMs deployed. As such, you should employ precision and stand-off weapons and restrict overflights of DRPK to only coastal areas.

Known DPRK Air Order of Battle:

  • Hwangju Air Base: Regiment MiG-21PFK Fishbed
  • Koksan Air Base: Regiment F-6A Farmer
  • Kwail Air Base Regiment F-5A Fresco
  • Pukchang Air Base Multiple MiG-23ML Regiments
  • Sunchon Air Base: Regiment MiG-29 Fulcrum

The current DPRK naval threat is a mix of few traditional assets such as frigates, gunboats, missile boats, artillery, coastal missile sites and submarines but also many asymmetric threats - from mines to frogmen to swarming combatants. Great care must be taken to protect your prepositioned assets.

Known DPRK Navy Bases:

  • Haeju Port: Small gunboats
  • Pipa Got Naval Base: Frigates, Submarines, Missile Boats, Small Combatants
  • Sagot Naval Base:Missile Boats, Small combatants
  • Cho Do Naval Base: Submarines, Missile Boats, Frigates, Small Combatants

Tactics displayed so far have been primarily defensive in nature although numerous artillery shellings have occurred within the last few weeks. We do expect these assets to be active once we cross the ROE threshhold.
