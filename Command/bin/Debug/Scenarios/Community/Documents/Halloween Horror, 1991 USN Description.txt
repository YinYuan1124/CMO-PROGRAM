SUBJ/URGENT… SET DEFCON THREE FOR ATLC COMMANDS//
1. AT 03:21 EDT SECSTATE, SECDEF, SECNAV AND CNO WERE INFORMED BY THE SOVIET AMBASSADOR TO THE UNITED STATES THAT THE SOVIET DELTA IV SUBMARINE K-84 IS ATTEMPTING TO CLOSE ON THE EASTERN CONUS AND THREATENS INDEPENDENT NUCLEAR LAUNCH.
2. CIA AND NAVAL INTELLIGENCE BELIEVE THE SUBMARINE IS LOCATED BETWEEN BERMUDA AND LABRADOR AND ATTEMPTING TO CLOSE ON EASTERN CONUS TRANSITING BETWEEN BERMUDA AND THE CONUS IN THE YANKEE BOX. NOTE A DELTA IV NEED NOT ENTER THE YANKEE BOX TO LAUNCH AND MAY REMAIN AT STANDOFF DISTANCE.
