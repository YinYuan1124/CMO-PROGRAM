We have identified some Syrian military aircraft!

Destroy them before they can get to Israel!

Note 1: You have some stealth aircraft under your command. They aircraft are harder to detect and to target, and thus can get closer to the enemy.

Note 2: The Syrian Fulcrums are armed with medium and long-range missiles and are more dangerous than the other aircraft. You can try to engage them with very long-range missiles, but this will probably result in a miss. Alternatively, you can use a stealth fighter to engage them at closer range.

Make sure to properly set the WRA for your forces in the Doctrine Window (CTRL + SHIFT + F9).
