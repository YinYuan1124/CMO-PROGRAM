Summary

In this tutorial you have used a wide array of munitions to attack various targets. Standard ‘dumb’ bombs tend to miss a lot but if you drop enough of them you will do some damage. More modern weapons, while not as versatile as a standard bomb do a much better job at hitting the targets. Remember that you should use the best tool for the job at hand – munition selection is one of the most important decisions you have when planning a strike.

There are no ‘wonder weapons’ that are all things to all targets; JDAMs are great if you know where the target is and its not moving; Mavericks are great against moving targets but don’t do a lot of damage; SDBs are great for small pinpoint targets but cannot penetrate and don’t hit very hard; Cluster bombs cover a wide area and penetrate armour but don’t do too much to buildings. The nearest thing that you have to a ‘Silver Bullet’ is a JASSM, but you probably won’t have many of them and only in very modern scenarios.
