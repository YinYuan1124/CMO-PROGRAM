General

Moscow has decided that we must act, and act quickly to solve several problems at once. We will rescue the people of Moldova from an oppressive dictatorship at the same time as we re-unite Novorossiya with Mother Russia. As a bonus we have an opportunity to show NATO as the 'Paper Tiger' that it has become by clearing the Black Sea of their forces and compelling their air forces to flee. We shall see if their 'Collective Defence policy can survive - if they are not unanimous in their decisions they will crumble - and we are working hard to make unanimity very difficult for them.

We have three main objectives:

  • Protect our land forces while they drive towards Moldova. They will complete this task in three phases: 1st by linking with our forces in the Crimea, 2nd by crossing the Dnieper River, and finally by forcing a corridor all the way to Moldova.
  • Sweep the Black Sea clear of NATO Naval forces, to do this you must gain Air superiority over the Crimea and the fleet.
  • Finally, show NATO weakness by destroying their new AEGIS Ashore system in Romania. This will be an attack directly at one of the centerpieces of their supposed and much vaunted 'Collective Defence', its destruction puts our nuclear option back on the table and should keep Europe in line.

The Ukrainian forces facing you're are a mix of very good to very poor elements. The job of your air force is to kill the very good elements and allow the Army to take care of the rest. You have some UAVs at Kursk and Rostov; and many close support aircraft to do so:

  • Primorsko Airbase: Su-25 & MiG-29s
  • Millerovo Airbase: MiG-29s
  • Buturlinovka Airbase: Su-25s
  • Lipetsk Airbase: Mig-29s

Another task the Army wishes of us, is to Destroy their various HQs and close the airbases. The rough areas where these HQs are located is marked on your map and you have some very good aircraft to do so:

  • Morozovsk Airbase: Su-24 & Su-34s
  • Voronezh Airbase: Su-24 & Su-34s
  • 448th Missile Bde: SS-26

To achieve Air superiority there are several Brigades and Regiments of Air Defence Missiles, and you have the following:

  • Krymsk Airbase: Su-27s
  • Akhtubinsk Airbase: Su-35s
  • Sennoy Airbase: MiG-31s
  • Efremov Airbase: 4x T-50 prototypes

To clear the NATO ships from the Black Sea you have the bulk of the Black Sea Fleet:

  • Gvardeyskoye Airbase: Su-24 & Su-30s
  • Balbek Airbase: ASW aviation
  • 11th Missile Brigade: SS-1, SS-5 & SS-6 SSMs
  • 11th ASW Brigade: led by the Moskva with 3 brand new FFGs
  • 295th Missile Boat Division: from Sevastopol with 4 Ships
  • 166th Missile Boat Division: from Novorossiysk with 6 ships, several of them are acting independently
  • 247th Submarine Division: 3 very new Kilo class SSKs
  • Several small boats including some Intelligence gathering ships shadowing NATO ships

To Destroy the AEGIS Ashore system, you have 3 Squadrons of Strategic Bombers at Engels Air Base. Use them wisely.

The 20th Guards Army will launch its assault at approximately 0800Zulu Time, Spetznaz forces are already deployed. You have from that time until they cross the frontier – probably about an hour and a half to find and destroy as many Ukrainian tanks and artillery units as you can. We expect some resistance from the Ukrainian air force – but not much! We anticipate that the operations against NATO will commence about 0830Zulu time, that gives you a short time to do some damage before NATO tries to interfere. Once the link up with the Crimean forces happens – other initiatives are in place that you will hear about.

Good luck General
