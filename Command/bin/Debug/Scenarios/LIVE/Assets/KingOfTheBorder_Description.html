Date: 28 October 2019
Location: Caribbean Sea, Colombia & Venezuela
Playable Sides: United States, Russia
Duration: 2 days

The King of the Border

The crisis that has enveloped Venezuela since 2013 has reached its boiling point.

The Government of Caracas has made assertions on several occasions that the reason for this humanitarian catastrophe is the constant interference of the United States of America and its puppets in the region conspiring to stop the glorious Bolivarian Revolution started by Hugo Chavez.

These suspicions have been reinforced by the blooming relationship between Colombia and NATO, culminating in 2015 in a joint military operation against maritime piracy.

Recently the situation has further escalated as two Colombian border towns, Cucuta and Arauca, and have been the focus of several skirmishes between soldiers wearing the uniform of The Bolivarian National Armed Forces of Venezuela and local Colombian border troops.

Claiming the episode is a machination of Washington to destroy the revolution, Venezuela has asked Russia for support, offering the Caribbean Island of Orchila as a permanent outpost. Moscow, eager to expand its political influence into South America, agreed immediately and has dispatched a joint expeditionary force in aid of its new ally.

The United States has declared its support for Bogota, promising protection from aggression by any means and have put their forces in Panama and Puerto Rico on the highest alert.
