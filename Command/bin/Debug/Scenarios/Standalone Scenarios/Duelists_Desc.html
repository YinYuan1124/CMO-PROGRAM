Original Concept By: Mike Mykytyn

Date: September 3rd 1989
Location: Norwegian Sea
Playable Sides: United Kingdom / USSR
Duration: 2 Days

The year is 1989 and the Soviet political strategy of bankrupting and breaking up the NATO alliance has been successful. America has pulled back into isolationism focusing on its domestic woes while Scandinavian and European nations rekindle pre-WW2 rivalries. The United Kingdom and West Germany now stand alone against the great Red threat. The Soviets move first by challenging Britain's mastery of the seas.
